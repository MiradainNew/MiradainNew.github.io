Our Mission

Built on Analytical
Independence

NAME Analytical is an independent quantitative research lab. We design and back-test investment methodologies across asset classes, delivering analysis that is rigorous, reproducible, and grounded in economic first principles.


Our work is governed by one discipline: let the data speak, and challenge it when it doesn't.

01
Quantitative Rigour
Every methodology is back-tested, stress-tested, and documented with full transparency of assumptions and limitations.
02

Models are anchored in macro-economic fundamentals, not curve-fitting. We distinguish signal from statistical artefact.
03
Institutional Delivery
Outputs are structured for investment committees, risk boards, and portfolio managers — decision-ready, not academic.

Methodology

Our Analytical
Process

01
Baseline Ingestion

We extract and reconcile raw track record data — monthly returns, volatility, drawdown profiles — establishing a behavioral baseline before any model is applied.

02
Regime Stress Testing

We isolate performance across macro stress events, revealing vulnerabilities concealed in benign market environments and establishing regime-conditional behavior profiles.

03
Factor Attribution

Our BMA framework decomposes return streams, distinguishing genuine alpha from systematic factor exposures with statistically robust confidence intervals.

04
Universe Benchmarking

Final placement within a curated peer group, mapping strategy positioning and differentiating alpha-generating managers from beta-heavy approaches.
